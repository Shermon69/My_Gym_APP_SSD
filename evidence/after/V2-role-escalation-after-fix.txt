--- 2. Verify assigned privileges ---

The test account was then authenticated using the normal signin endpoint.

Observed result:

Result:
The attacker-controlled "roles":["admin"] value was no longer assigned
to the newly created account.

The signup controller now initializes new users with an empty roles array
and no longer assigns roles based on req.body.roles.

The same privilege-escalation attempt therefore fails after the fix.
