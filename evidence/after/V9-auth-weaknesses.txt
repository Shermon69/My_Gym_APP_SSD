--- Observation ---
The account has 10 login attempts per 15 minutes. Once that budget is used up,
the server starts rejecting further attempts with 429 Too Many Requests instead
of even checking the password - a brute-force script can no longer run at full
speed against a single account.

--- 4. Password hashing cost (source evidence) ---
server/src/api/controllers/auth.controller.js - both bcrypt.hashSync() calls
now use BCRYPT_COST = 12 instead of the hardcoded 8.

Note: the rate limiter above tracks attempts by IP address, not by username.
That is the standard express-rate-limit behaviour and is a reasonable tradeoff
for a small app - worth a one-line mention in the report as a known limitation
(e.g. many users behind the same office network share one limit).
