--- Observation ---
Step 1 returns 404 'User Not found.' -- Step 2 returns 401 'Invalid Password!'
The two failure modes are distinguishable, so an attacker can enumerate valid
usernames by the status code and message alone, with no rate limiting to slow
down the enumeration or a subsequent credential-stuffing attempt.
