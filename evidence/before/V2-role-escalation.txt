=== V2 - Role Escalation During Signup - BEFORE evidence ===

Baseline commit: d0ee685

--- 1. Privilege escalation exploit ---

Request:
POST /api/auth/signup

The signup request supplied an attacker-controlled role:

roles: ["admin"]

Test account:
username: v2adminpoc927
email: v2adminpoc927@example.com

Server response:
User was registered successfully!

--- 2. Verify assigned privileges ---

The test account was then authenticated using the normal signin endpoint.

Observed result:
roles: {ROLE_ADMIN}

Result:
The attacker-controlled "roles":["admin"] value was accepted during
registration and the newly created account received ROLE_ADMIN.

This demonstrates the V2 role-escalation vulnerability in the baseline.

--- 3. Safety and credential handling ---

The test was performed against the local development environment.

The JWT access token returned by signin was intentionally excluded
from this evidence file.
